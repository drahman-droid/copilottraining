Program Instructions

All staff are required to complete one training module per week over the next three consecutive weeks using the Microsoft Learning Centre. Each module takes approximately 30 minutes.

  • Click the relevant week button below to access your training module.
  • You will be redirected to the Microsoft Learning Centre — sign in with your Microsoft work account before starting.
  • Complete the module in full (approx. 30 minutes).
  • At your weekly performance meeting, demonstrate completion to your manager.
  • Managers must verify that their team members have completed each weekly module.

How to show proof of completion
  1. Go to Microsoft Learning Centre
  2. Click your account icon (top right corner)
  3. Select Profile
  4. Click Training
  5. Show your manager the completed module
Manager responsibilities
  • Ensure all direct reports complete their module each week.
  • Review proof of completion at weekly performance meetings.
  • Escalate any non-completion concerns to your HR business partner.

Sign-in reminder: When you click a week button above, you will be redirected to the Microsoft Learning Centre. Please sign in using your Microsoft work account before beginning the module. Do not use a personal Microsoft account.
